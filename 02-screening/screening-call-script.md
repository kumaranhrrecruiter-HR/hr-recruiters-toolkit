Start with a short introduction:

“Hello, am I speaking with [Candidate Name]? My name is [Recruiter Name], and I'm calling regarding the [Job Title] opportunity at [Company Name]. Is this a convenient time for a brief conversation?”

Then cover:
1. Can you briefly describe your relevant experience?
2. Which skills or projects best match this position?
3. What are you looking for in your next role?
4. What is your notice period or earliest joining date?
5. Does the role's location and work arrangement suit you?
6. Does the approved compensation range align with your expectations?

Close by explaining the next step and when the candidate can expect an update. Never promise selection before the evaluation is complete.
