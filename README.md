# HR Recruiter's Toolkit

A practical recruitment documentation project demonstrating my understanding of the end-to-end hiring process.

## Project Overview

This portfolio contains structured recruitment documents designed to support consistent, professional, and evidence-based hiring.

The examples use a fictional cybersecurity company and fictional job requirements.

## Objectives

- Understand the end-to-end recruitment lifecycle.
- Standardize candidate screening and interview evaluation.
- Improve candidate communication and follow-up.
- Support a smooth onboarding process.
- Promote fair and job-related hiring decisions.

## Recruitment Workflow

1. Receive and review the job description.
2. Clarify role requirements with the hiring manager.
3. Identify suitable sourcing channels.
4. Review resumes against mandatory criteria.
5. Conduct initial screening calls.
6. Coordinate interviews.
7. Collect structured interview feedback.
8. Coordinate the offer process.
9. Complete pre-boarding and onboarding activities.
