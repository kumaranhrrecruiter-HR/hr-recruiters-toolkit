# Standard Operating Procedure: Recruitment

**Document ID:** HR-SOP-001  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Purpose
To establish a consistent recruitment process that supports timely hiring, candidate communication and job-related evaluation.

## 2. Scope
Applies to the recruitment lifecycle, from receiving an approved job requisition through candidate onboarding coordination.

## 3. Responsibilities
- **Recruiter:** Reviews requirements, sources candidates, screens applicants and coordinates interviews.
- **Hiring manager:** Validates role requirements and evaluates job-related competencies.
- **HR team:** Coordinates offer documentation and onboarding activities.
- **Candidate:** Provides accurate information and required documents.

## 4. Recruitment procedure
| Stage | Recruiter action | Expected output |
|---|---|---|
| Requirement intake | Review JD and clarify mandatory skills | Approved role criteria |
| Sourcing | Identify suitable candidates through authorized channels | Candidate pipeline |
| Screening | Compare experience and skills with requirements | Screening records |
| Initial call | Confirm relevant experience, interest and logistics | Screening notes |
| Interview | Schedule interviews and share instructions | Interview schedule |
| Evaluation | Collect structured feedback | Documented assessment |
| Offer | Coordinate approved offer communication | Offer status |
| Onboarding | Coordinate joining requirements | Onboarding checklist |

## 5. Quality controls
- Apply the same relevant criteria consistently.
- Record evidence rather than relying on assumptions.
- Keep candidate information confidential.
- Obtain approval before communicating formal offers.
- Follow up with candidates at appropriate stages.

## 6. Performance measures
- Time-to-fill
- Screening-to-interview conversion
- Interview-to-offer conversion
- Offer acceptance rate
- Candidate communication turnaround time

## 7. Review
Review the procedure periodically and update it when approved processes or organizational requirements change.

*This is a sample learning document, not an approved employer policy.*
